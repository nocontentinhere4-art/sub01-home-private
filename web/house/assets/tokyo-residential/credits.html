Tokyo panorama

“Memorial for Tokiwa-so apartment house” by Masato OHTA (heiwa4126), photographed December 6, 2009 in Toshima, Tokyo. Source and reviewed license.

Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 2.0. Resized to 4096×2048 / 2048×1024 and encoded WebP. Brightness and color are graded by the simulated clock.

This is a historical static photograph, not a live Tokyo feed, a separate night capture, or navigable city geometry. Nearby paving and the house are original geometry. The photographer does not endorse this project.
