## question-ik.txt

Design review before a native Unreal Engine 5.8.3 build (Linux, Vulkan, RTX 4090, game thread + render thread + RHI thread). Please look for real flaws, ranked by severity, with the concrete fix. Be specific; say "no issue" where the code is right. One attached excerpt (excerpt-ik.cpp) (C++ generated by our installers; the code compiles against a stub, not yet against the engine).

CONTEXT
A seated streamer character (Mixamo skeleton: LeftArm/LeftForeArm/LeftHand, LeftHandIndex1..3 etc.) plays authored desk loops. A custom FAnimInstanceProxy evaluates the pose; after breathing/posture and before the head offset, our own two-bone IK moves the wrists onto targets on the keyboard (world targets converted to component space by the character every tick, copied to the proxy in PreUpdate). The reviewed hands recipe: wrists 4-5 cm above the desk, home-row wrists 18-22 cm apart, elbows 85-100 deg interior, elbow poles down/back/slightly out, neutral wrist, finger curl 20-35 deg proximal / 25-40 deg middle, right wrist to the mouse in 0.25 s on a 4 cm arc, left hand planted. The mesh component carries a "seat offset" relative transform (yaw twist + translation) so component space != the clip's original space. The keyboard is an axis-aligned static mesh; he faces about -Y in world, yaw 165 deg.

Owner requirement for the second camera ("cam2"): a view from behind him showing his back, the desk and monitor, AT THE SAME TIME as the main view (the main view is the "webcam"); 1280x720, 60 fps target, the game itself must stay at 60 fps; never block the game thread; output = whole JPEG frames back to back into a FIFO that ffmpeg reads with -f mjpeg (the reader may be absent or slow: drop whole frames, never block, never split a JPEG). A status JSON every second, a log line every 30 s with achieved fps and game-thread cost.

QUESTIONS
1. Two-bone solve (excerpt-ik.cpp, StreamerV2SolveTwoBone and EvaluateStreamerV2Hands): is the math right (law of cosines, bend plane from the clip with a pole bias, minimal rotation for the upper arm, forearm carried by D1 then bent by D2 so the forearm does not roll, local = parent^-1 * component, slerp of local rotations by alpha)? Any twist/flip risk when the target crosses the shoulder axis or the pole is nearly parallel to the shoulder-target axis? Is slerping LOCAL rotations by alpha acceptable or should the blend be done on component-space positions?
2. Is it correct that the IK runs on the compact pose in component space computed by walking parents (StreamerV2ComponentTransform) each time (cost ~ a few hundred transform multiplies per frame)? Any problem with bones missing from the compact pose (LOD), or with the 0.5 elbow pole weight (down 1, back 0.5, out 0.3 in his frame)?
3. Target placement (UpdateStreamerVisualHands): wrist = keyboard bounds centre + 13 cm towards him + (desk + 4.5 cm) height, +-10 cm sideways; mouse wrist = mouse centre + 7 cm towards him. Are 13 cm and 7 cm plausible for Mixamo-proportioned hands (wrist to middle fingertip about 18-19 cm) with the fingers on the home row / palm on the mouse? The glide: eased 0.25 s with a 4 cm sine arc, restarted when the goal jumps > 2 cm, tracked when it drifts less. Any flaw?
4. Finger curl: additive local rotation about one local axis (X/Y/Z chosen by a tuning knob) on phalanges 1 and 2, measured flexion logged. Is the measurement (angle between hand->finger1 and finger1->finger2 for the knuckle; finger1->finger2 vs finger2->finger3 for the middle joint) a sensible proxy?

## question-posefix.txt

Design review before a native Unreal Engine 5.8.3 build: the fixes for the visual QA of our seated streamer character (Mixamo skeleton imported into UE: Hips, Spine, Spine1, Spine2, Neck, Head, LeftUpLeg/RightUpLeg, arms...). Excerpt attached (excerpt-posefix.cpp, generated C++; compiles against a stub, the engine build is running). Please find real flaws, ranked, with the concrete fix; say "ok" where right.

FACTS FROM THE QA (measured on build v19)
- Offsets written as FRotator(Pitch,0,0) and post-multiplied into the LOCAL rotation of Spine1/Spine2/Head rotate about each bone's long (twist) axis on this import: the "sit-up" lean twisted the torso, the reacting nod was a head shake, gaze pitch turned the head left/right. You (Kimi) recommended: a_local = normalize(inverse(Q) * R) with R the character's right vector in component space, lean spread over the spine chain, nod on neck+head, acceptance +10 deg lean -> pelvis-to-head inclination +10 +- 1 deg (pelvis < 1 mm, lateral head drift < 5 mm), +5 deg nod -> 5 +- 0.5 deg face pitch, < 1 deg yaw, < 1 deg twist.
- The desk loops (~5.5 s) pop the head 7-9 cm at every loop wrap (end and start poses differ).
- The face camera re-aimed on every switch to it (1 s noisy head mean, ~4 s to settle).
- He faces ~17 deg off the monitor; the loops look ~20 deg left of the lens.
- The chair backrest passes through his back (white frame visible through the hoodie) at seat pull-back 36 cm: chair and body move together, the relation is wrong; recipe: backrest 0-2 cm behind the sacrum.

WHAT THE EXCERPT DOES
1. Body frame from the pose each evaluation: Right = flattened (RightUpLeg - LeftUpLeg) in component space (fallback -X), Forward = Right x Up. Each bone turns in COMPONENT space about its own pivot: local' = parent^-1 * Delta * parent * local (equivalent to your a_local formula). Lean (FORWARD positive; the tuning "posture_lean_deg -16" means sit up) spread Spine 0.5 / Spine1 0.3 / Spine2 0.2 with a gain adapted every frame (gain <- gain + 0.2*(clamp(gain*cmd/achieved, 0.5, 3) - gain)) so the measured pelvis(Hips)->Head inclination change equals the command; breathing 0.6 deg on Spine1/Spine2 after the measurement. Head channel: pitch (nose UP positive) and yaw (to his RIGHT positive), 0.4 Neck / 0.6 Head, Delta = Yaw(Up) * Pitch(about Right, -angle). Sign check we derived: FQuat(Right,+a) applied to Up tilts it towards Forward = Right x Up (UE cross product, left-handed world, UE yaw positive turns right).
2. Loop seam: in PreUpdate, when the looping clip's time + dt reaches length - fade (fade = min(loop_fade_s 0.5, 25% of length)), Previous takes the same clip at the current time (non-looping, advanced by the existing captured-layer clock), Current restarts at 0, Progress = 0 and Update advances Progress over the fade instead of the 0.24 s state-change crossfade. Blend.Alpha = Progress (two-way blend Previous -> Current).
3. Face camera: an exponential mean (tau 5 s) of the Head bone updated every tick while v2 runs (any camera); aim once at the first selection (>= 2 s of samples), keep the aim across camera switches, re-aim only on a tuning change (after a fresh mean).
4. Seat yaw: Seat = ClipOffset * YawAbout(pivot = R9's seated pelvis, component space) * PullBack, applied as the mesh component's relative transform; chair parts turn about the same vertical (world pivot = authored body transform * pivot) and translate with the pull-back.
5. Chair fit: backrest candidates = chair parts whose bounds centre is >= 3 cm behind the pelvis along his facing, reaching >= 15 cm above the pelvis, within 20 cm sideways; front = max over their AABBs of the support along the facing; sacrum = pelvis bone - facing * 11 cm; move the chair along the facing until (sacrum - front) = 1 cm; passes 1 s apart after the seat settles, max 4 per tuning change, clamp +-25 cm, deadband 0.5 cm.

QUESTIONS
A. Is the per-bone turn formula and the sign convention right? Any hazard of deriving Right from the thighs every frame (the clips move the legs; a seated pose has thighs pointing forward: is RightUpLeg-LeftUpLeg still a good lateral axis, or should it come from the Hips bone rotation or the shoulders)? Does the adaptive gain risk oscillation or a runaway with breathing/clip motion? Is spreading the yaw over Neck+Head fine?
B. Loop seam: any flaw with reusing the state-change players (e.g. Previous non-looping clamps at the end, the phase-matching code, the seat offset blend, a state change arriving mid-seam)? Is 0.5 s a sensible default for a 7-9 cm mismatch?
C. Face aim: any flaw?
D. Seat yaw/chair turn/chair fit: any flaw (order of transforms, AABB support of a reclined backrest, armrest exclusion, convergence, chair jumping during the stream)? What sacrum offset from a Mixamo Hips bone is plausible (we use 11 cm behind)?
E. Anything that would misbehave at runtime or break the UE 5.8 build.

Answer in English, concise (at most ~900 words), as a list A-E with severities (blocker / should fix / nit / ok). Keep the reasoning short.
