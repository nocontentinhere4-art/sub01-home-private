NOT applied: your cam2 default "manual, bias -3". Our reasoning: with AutoExposureApplyPhysicalCameraExposure off, manual mode
fixes the exposure at the engine's EV100 base, so the bias is the absolute log2 scale; the house's histogram volume pins this
dim interior at its EV100 -3 floor with bias 1, i.e. scale 2^(1+3) = 16 (the same arithmetic our older daylight-exposure tool
uses: "minEV -3 / bias 1 caps scale at 16"). Manual -3 would be 2^-3, ~7 EV darker than the webcam: black. We kept mode 1
(auto + bias) at -1.0 (a relative -2 EV in every eye-adaptation state) as the default; manual stays available live (+4 would
match the webcam, +2 two stops darker). Please confirm or refute this EV arithmetic for UE 5.8 manual mode.
