# SUB-01 streamer v1: what runs, how to drive it, what was verified (2026-10-23)

## The result in one paragraph
SUB-01 sits at his desk in the native Unreal home on the pod, a real Chromium desktop on a dedicated display is his
PC, a Claude-driven loop observes the screen, decides, acts, verifies the outcome and comments with his own voice
(Chatterbox, voice K07), the public chat of the site reaches him in batches and he answers by voice, and a local
director records the character scene with the desktop in a picture-in-picture. A 30-minute rehearsal ran end to end
with an induced browser crash and an induced model error, both recovered without manual help.

## How to start and stop (on the pod, as root)
```
S=/home/unreal/.sub01-private/latest-source-20261023
P=/home/unreal/.sub01-private/streamer
T=/home/unreal/.sub01-private/operator-token
# 1. backend streamer hold (pins him at the desk, pauses walks/phone/bed/TV/outside/day plan)
curl -s -X POST -H "X-Admin-Token: $(cat $T)" -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
     --data '{"operation":"start"}' http://127.0.0.1:8830/api/home/streamer
# 2. all stream services: Xvfb :102, desktop runner (8832), voice (8835), agent (8836), director (8837)
runuser -u unreal -- /home/unreal/venv/bin/python $S/scripts/streamer_up.py start --source $S --streamer-dir $P \
     --token-file $T --child-env "director=XAUTHORITY=/home/unreal/.sub01-private/native-xauthority" \
     --child-arg "director=--game-pid-file $P/game.pid"
runuser -u unreal -- /home/unreal/venv/bin/python $S/scripts/streamer_up.py status --source $S --streamer-dir $P --token-file $T
# kill switch: autonomous actions and speech stop within a second, scene stays up (desktop, voice, director)
runuser -u unreal -- /home/unreal/venv/bin/python $S/scripts/streamer_up.py kill   --source $S --streamer-dir $P --token-file $T
runuser -u unreal -- /home/unreal/venv/bin/python $S/scripts/streamer_up.py resume --source $S --streamer-dir $P --token-file $T
# full stop
runuser -u unreal -- /home/unreal/venv/bin/python $S/scripts/streamer_up.py stop   --source $S --streamer-dir $P --token-file $T
curl -s -X POST -H "X-Admin-Token: $(cat $T)" -H 'Content-Type: application/json' --data '{"operation":"stop"}' http://127.0.0.1:8830/api/home/streamer
```
The native game must run with `-Sub01StreamerVisual` (launcher `tools/pod_live/launch-streamer-game-20261023.py`
pattern); `$P/game.pid` holds its pid for the director. Recordings: `$P/recordings/*.mp4` (10-minute segments).
Logs: `$P/logs/*.log`, decisions and outcomes in `$P/events.jsonl`, speech in `$P/speech/timeline.jsonl`.
Rehearsal driver: `scripts/streamer_rehearsal.py --minutes 30`; highlights: `scripts/streamer_highlights.py`.

## Native animation at the desk
- v2 of the streamer visual (compiled on the pod, build v10/v12): seated loop (`SupportedWebsite`) for idle/reading/
  talking/reacting, `SupportedDeskTyping` for typing, breathing on the spine, head sway, gaze towards the screen
  position of what he reads or clicks, one nod on reactions, 0.24 s cross-fades, event-driven from the agent
  (`streamer-visual-event.json` v2).
- `mouse` (AuthoredReach) moved the body out of frame: clicks keep the reading pose until the clip is reviewed.
- Face camera (frontal webcam framing, the owner's reference) is implemented and compiled (build v11) but its
  desk-pin guard displaced the body; a corrected patch is in progress. Until then the spectator camera is used.
- No lip sync or facial expression: the mesh has no face rig. Talking shows as head rhythm and posture only.

## Known limits (declared)
- Chat only from the site's own WebSocket; no Twitch/YouTube/Kick connector yet.
- Non-actionable chat lines still get the old canned reaction in the room ("Not doing that one..."); the voice
  answer is the real reply. To be softened in the backend.
- No application audio in the recording (no PulseAudio on the pod); voice only.
- Hard cuts between layouts; the live composite is the character layout with desktop PiP, `layout.jsonl` keeps
  the agent's layout choices for a post pass.
- The in-game monitor texture refreshes about twice a second; the desktop PiP is the readable view.
- The final PC will be Vagon Streams; today's desktop is the Chromium runner with the same interfaces.
- The API key was pasted in chat by the owner: rotate it when convenient and replace the file on the pod.
