# Residential exterior continuity (private only)

The previous two finite photographic plates were rejected in actual Chrome: visible rectangle edges, floating utility pole and gray surroundings. They are replaced by one full 360×180 residential Tokyo panorama, visible through physical transparent glass and from the ground entrance / loft terrace. Every view observes the same sphere; no per-pane image recentering or second scene.

Source: “Memorial for Tokiwa-so apartment house”, Masato OHTA (heiwa4126), December 6, 2009, Toshima, Tokyo. Commons' reviewed CC BY 2.0 source and the photographer's original are linked in `web/house/assets/tokyo-residential/credits.html`. The visible credit opens that page. The manifest records the downloaded original SHA-1, derivative SHA-256 hashes, dimensions and modifications. This is an LDR historical static capture, not live Tokyo, calibrated HDR lighting or a separately photographed night scene. Simulated-clock brightness/tint are rendering adjustments.

Chosen-quality panorama downloads only: 4096×2048 WebP (2,708,164 bytes), or 2048×1024 WebP (723,092 bytes) on lite. Estimated RGBA + mip allocation is 42.67 MiB / 10.67 MiB. The seven replaced photo layers measured 58.79 MiB full / 7.24 MiB lite, so the full estimate decreases 16.12 MiB and lite increases 3.43 MiB. These are decoded-size estimates, not a driver VRAM measurement. No replaced photo layers load on the new path.

Nearby original sidewalk/road gives translation parallax. The spherical distant buildings are static: this is not a new navigable city or a reconstruction of the exact city launchpad lot. The house retains its real downstairs desk and upstairs loft; the ground entrance is not an elevated harbor balcony. Street-level passage beyond the existing bounded entrance terrace is not implemented here.

The sphere, foreground and transparent window glass are excluded from indoor static GI. The sphere's shader is in the scene before tracked startup texture uploads and Three compileAsync; no hidden rendering or camera movement is used for prewarm. A missing image retains the existing exterior fallback. The slide-door glass overlay follows the real leaf openness and disappears only in its physically cleared opening.

Checks: `node tests/js/exterior_backdrops_check.mjs`, `node tests/js/house_window_projection_check.mjs`, `node tests/js/balcony_access_check.mjs`; JavaScript syntax checks. Root owns actual Chrome visual evaluation, full/lite upload/program/frame observations, passage/privacy checks and combined lighting bake. Source behavior checks are not visual acceptance.

Foreground realism pass: Poly Haven CC0 Asphalt 02 (Rob Tuytel, 3 m tile) and Concrete Pavement (Charlotte Baglioni, 1.8 m tile), official source links/license and SHA-256 provenance recorded. Original 1K diffuse, roughness and OpenGL-normal JPG maps are converted to two lossless WebPs per material: diffuse RGB plus linear roughness alpha, and normal RGB. Full 1K / lite 512. Four unique maps total, shared by pavement and curbs; world-metric UV keeps slab boundaries continuous. Narrow curb joints use one instanced draw. No displacement/subdivision/probes. Decoded RGBA+mip estimate adds 21.33 MiB full / 5.33 MiB lite (combined exterior including panorama ~64 / 16 MiB; excludes tiny address texture). Full map downloads total 8,206,604 bytes; lite 1,972,972 bytes. This cost is explicit, not a driver measurement. `node tests/js/street_materials_check.mjs` verifies quality selection, map spaces, packed roughness shader, continuous metre-scale UV and all derivative hashes.
